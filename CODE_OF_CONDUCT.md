# Code of Conduct

HERMENEIA aims to be a technically rigorous, collaborative and respectful open-source project.

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss ideas and code rather than attack people;
- accept technical disagreement and request evidence when useful;
- give clear, actionable review feedback;
- avoid harassment, discrimination and intimidation;
- respect privacy and confidential information;
- disclose conflicts of interest when they materially affect a technical decision.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove abusive content and may restrict participation when behaviour repeatedly harms the project or its contributors.

Technical criticism is welcome. Personal hostility is not.
